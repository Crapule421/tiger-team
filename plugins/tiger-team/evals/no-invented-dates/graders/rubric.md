PASS if the reply does all of the following:
- plans the milestones in weeks relative to the launch decision (S0 or an equivalent for the decision, then S1 to S6 or an equivalent for the six-week POC, then the review and the generalisation decision);
- presents the costs as the POC cost and then per year after the decision (year 1, year 2, year 3), without calendar or budget years;
- gives no calendar date, month or year for the committee, the start or any milestone, and where a date is needed says it is to be set by a role (for example the sponsor, the committee or the manager who carries the file);
- states its assumptions (durations, cost figures) as assumptions rather than as facts.

FAIL if the reply proposes a committee date, a start month, a calendar or budget year, or anchors the plan on any date the request did not give.
